## Recurring characters
- Tessa Marsh: Chicago home stager who inherits her late uncle Len Marsh's staging business on Quarry Road, Larkspur Lake, Wisconsin.
- Owen Reyes: local realtor, holds forty cents of every net dollar under the 2023 note and the placemat terms.
- Moose: Len's golden retriever.
- Bev Halvorsen: retired librarian and neighbor on Birch Street.
- Gus Pelletier: owner of the Dock Street Diner.
- Hank Dressler and son Eli Dressler: Dressler Marine, the back field neighbor.
- Joyce Kessler: township zoning officer (Village of Larkspur Lake, Office of Zoning and Land Use).
- Diane Marsh: Tessa's mother.
- Lucy Ambrose: Tessa's Chicago friend.
- Brent Malloy: Chicago competitor turned referral partner (Northline Staging, Milwaukee).
