## Description (paste as is)
Tessa Marsh came to Larkspur Lake, Wisconsin, to close out her late uncle's home-staging business and go back to Chicago. On her first morning in town, she pulls into a lakeside cottage on Birch Street and finds a rival already unloading the same cream sofa she has brought for the same open house. The rival is Owen Reyes, a local realtor who has been quietly borrowing her uncle's props for two years. The two agree to split the job, and they immediately start to bicker over every lamp, rug, and throw pillow. Between a bad open house, a storage unit full of tagged treasures, and a notebook that reveals a secret deal her uncle never explained, their rivalry turns into a partnership, and then into something neither of them planned. With a buyer's offer on the line, a storm that tests the roof and the heart, and a town that has opinions about everything, Tessa has to decide whether to sell her uncle's business and leave, or stay and build something of her own. Witty, warm, and full of small-town charm, Staged for Love is a contemporary romantic comedy with a close-knit cast of neighbors, a loyal golden retriever, and a clear happy ending. It is the first book in the Larkspur Lake Romantic Comedy series.
