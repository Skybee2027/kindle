## Description (paste as is)
When Greta Lowe, a semi-retired court reporter, takes down the minutes of a contentious Lantern Bay town council meeting in July, she is the only person in the room who writes down everything. The next morning, the council's harbor commissioner, Dale Whitcomb, is found dead at the bottom of the concrete steps behind the Harbor Office. The official story is an accident. Greta's steno tape says otherwise. The minutes that were filed that night describe a vote that never happened, a sentence that was never spoken, and a recess that the tape shows was silent for fourteen minutes. Someone has rewritten the record of the meeting, and that someone has a reason to want Dale quiet. With her cat, Judge, sitting on every document she touches, and her friend Priya Rao pushing her toward the questions nobody else wants to ask, Greta begins to unravel a web of comfortable lies that stretches from the town council chamber to a lakeside development deal worth millions. Along the way she must decide how much of the truth a small town can bear, and whether a woman who has spent thirty-one years letting the record speak for itself can finally be the one who speaks. Shorthand for Murder is a clean, character-driven cozy mystery with a close-knit cast, a lakeside setting, and a puzzle that rewards careful readers. It is the first book in the Lantern Bay Cozy Mystery series.
